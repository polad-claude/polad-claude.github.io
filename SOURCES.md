# POLAD Claude — source directory

Reference list read by the daily task. X (Twitter) and Truth Social cannot be read directly, so every leader is caught either on an **official website** (preferred, `sourceType: official`) or through a **relay outlet** that reprints posts within the hour (`sourceType: media`, quote label "posted on X" / "posted on Truth Social").

Names of office-holders are deliberately not listed: they change. Resolve the current head of state, head of government and main opposition leader(s) at run time from the official site or relay outlet.

Legend: **HoS** head of state · **HoG** head of government · **X-first** leader who usually announces on social media before any official site — search the relay outlets for them.

## Opposition

Opposition leaders rarely have an official site: catch them through the relay outlets above, searching in the national language. Include an opposition statement only when it concerns NATO, the EU, Ukraine, Russia, defence spending or deployments **and** is significant: a formal party position, a parliamentary vote announcement, a call that could change government policy, or a statement during an election campaign. Routine criticism is excluded.
